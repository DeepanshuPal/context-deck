WATCH IN YOUR BROWSER

YouTube & Netflix

  1. Click Show extension folder below. Finder opens with a folder called Browser Extension.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Drag the Browser Extension folder onto that page (or click Load unpacked and pick it).
  4. Play a YouTube or Netflix video with subtitles on. Hover any word for its definition, press S to save it here.

YouTube cards get a picture and the line's audio. Netflix is copy-protected, so its cards are text with a link back to the moment. Keep this app open while you watch; words saved while it's closed wait in Chrome and arrive when you open it.
